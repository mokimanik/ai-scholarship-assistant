OFFICIAL POLICY DOCUMENT: MERIT-CUM-MEANS NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP (ID: SCH001)

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
The Merit-cum-Means National Scholarship (SCH001) is designed to assist economically disadvantaged undergraduate students pursuing higher education. Applicants must be enrolled in full-time accredited degree programs across Year 1 to Year 4. The applicant must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 7.5 on a 10.0 scale. Furthermore, the total annual family income from all sources must not exceed INR 5,00,000 per annum. This scheme is open to students of all social categories.

2. APPLICATION PROCESS AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Applications must be submitted electronically through the Central Scholarship Portal before October 31, 2026. Applicants must upload scanned copies of mandatory documents:
- Valid Annual Family Income Certificate issued by a competent revenue officer.
- Official academic Mark Sheet / Grade Card of the preceding semester or board examination.
- Aadhaar Card for identity and bank account linkage verification.
Incomplete applications or submissions received after the deadline will be automatically rejected.

3. VERIFICATION AND SELECTION
All submissions undergo a two-tier verification process consisting of institutional verification by the college dean and final auditing by the State Scholarship Board. Merit rank lists are generated based on combined CGPA and financial need weighting.

4. APPEAL AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
If an applicant believes their application was rejected in error, an official appeal may be lodged with the Grievance Redressal Cell within 15 calendar days of result publication. Appeals must include supporting evidence and documentary proof. The Decision of the Appellate Board shall be final and binding.

5. RENEWAL CONDITIONS
Scholarship renewal is subject to satisfactory academic progress. Beneficiaries must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.5 in annual examinations and submit an updated annual income statement. Failure to maintain academic standards or submission of fraudulent documentation will lead to immediate cancellation and financial recovery.
