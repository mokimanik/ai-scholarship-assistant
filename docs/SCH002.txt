OFFICIAL POLICY DOCUMENT: POST-MATRIC SC/ST EMPOWERMENT GRANT (ID: SCH002)

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
The Post-Matric SC/ST Empowerment Grant (SCH002) aims to provide financial support to students belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC) communities. Applicants must be enrolled in recognized post-matric or higher education programs (Years 1 to 4). Applicants are required to have secured a minimum CGPA of 6.0 in their qualifying examination. The maximum gross annual family income ceiling is set at INR 2,50,000 per annum.

2. APPLICATION PROCESS AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Eligible candidates must complete the online application process on the Welfare Portal prior to November 15, 2026. Required supporting documents include:
- Official Caste/Community Certificate verified by the District Social Welfare Department.
- Income Certificate valid for the current financial year.
- Copy of Bank Passbook showing active Aadhaar-seeded bank account details.
All uploaded files must be clear and legibly scanned in PDF format.

3. VERIFICATION AND DISBURSEMENT
Applications are verified against state demographic databases. Approved grant amounts (up to INR 75,000) are disbursed directly to student bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in two installments.

4. APPEAL AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL PROCESS
Applicants facing rejection due to document discrepancies may submit a rectification request through the Online Grievance Portal within 20 days. Physical verification may be requested at the District Welfare Office. Decisions made by the Welfare Commissioner are non-negotiable.

5. RENEWAL AND CONTINUATION CONDITIONS
To continue receiving the grant in subsequent academic years, students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% and pass all enrolled courses with a minimum CGPA of 6.0. Grant continuation requests must be endorsed annually by the institute head.
