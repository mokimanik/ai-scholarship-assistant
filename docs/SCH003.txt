OFFICIAL POLICY DOCUMENT: SENIOR TECH EXCELLENCE FELLOWSHIP (ID: SCH003)

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
The Senior Tech Excellence Fellowship (SCH003) recognizes high-achieving undergraduate students in technology and engineering disciplines. Only students in their 2nd or 3rd academic year are eligible to apply. Applicants must demonstrate exceptional academic performance with a minimum CGPA of 8.5. The total annual family income limit for applicants is INR 8,00,000 per annum. This fellowship is open to students across all social categories.

2. APPLICATION PROCEDURE AND DOCUMENTATION
Candidates must submit their application package on the Tech Excellence Portal no later than December 1, 2026. Required documents comprise:
- Comprehensive Curriculum Vitae (Resume) highlighting academic achievements and technical projects.
- One official Recommendation Letter from a faculty member or research supervisor.
- Certified copy of the official University Transcript.
Late or incomplete submissions will not be evaluated under any circumstances.

3. EVALUATION AND FELLOWSHIP AWARD
Selection is based on a cumulative score evaluating academic transcript strength (60%), recommendation quality (20%), and technical project potential (20%). Awardees receive a financial grant of INR 1,00,000 along with mentorship access.

4. APPEAL AND RE-EVALUATION POLICY
Re-evaluation requests regarding candidate scoring may be filed within 10 days of shortlist announcement by sending a formal petition to the Selection Committee. Re-evaluations are limited to administrative audit of scoring calculations.

5. RENEWAL AND MILESTONE CONDITIONS
Fellowship extension requires maintaining a 8.5 CGPA and submitting a mid-year technical project progress report. Failure to meet academic thresholds or progress milestones will result in forfeiture of remaining disbursement tranches.
